# VisionCut AI Vision

VisionCut AI is not only a video editor.

It is an AI editing partner.

The AI should understand:

- storytelling
- viewer retention
- hooks
- pacing
- cinematic composition
- real estate marketing


Future AI abilities:

## Hook Detection

Analyze videos and recommend the strongest first 5 seconds.


## B-roll Selection

Find the most attractive shots automatically.


## Music Assistant

Recommend music based on:

- mood
- pacing
- property style
- audience


## SFX Assistant

Suggest sound effects that improve retention.


## Auto Editing

Generate social media ready videos.


The AI should think like a professional editor, not only process files.
